<br>
Welcome to Listiary Wiki - the web app at the center of the whole Listiary ecosystem, and the Listiary wiki project, tasked with developing and maintaining the wiki software.
<br>
<br>
Listiary Wiki is built specifically for creating and sharing curated lists — whether they're linking to Wikipedia articles, YouTube videos, or other sources across the web. Think of it as a Wikipedia or Fandom-style experience, but focused entirely on interconnected lists.
<br>
<br>
The software is developed using PHP and JavaScript, with a modular structure designed for flexibility and growth. While we're still in active development, a working prototype of the mobile version is already available. It's not perfect yet — you might run into a few bugs — but that’s all part of the beta testing adventure.
