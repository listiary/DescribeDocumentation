<br>
Subproject Social Outreach is created to guide and aid the process of sharing the Listiary Wiki with the world.

In this section of the documentation (subpr. Social Outreach), contributors can find copies of published posts and articles about Listiary, content to help write new materials, various guidelines, previous outreach effort summaries, and more. Over time, this space will also track our PR milestones and community engagement progress.

The goal of this sub-project is to help us stay consistent and intentional in how we present Listiary, while making our life easier in the PR domain.

This is a work in progress.<br>
