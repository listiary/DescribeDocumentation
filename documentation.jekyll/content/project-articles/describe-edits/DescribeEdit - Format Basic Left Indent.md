## Description:
We sometimes mess the indent of lists.
By using a basic algorithm, without even parsing the document, we can look at lines that end in production arrow, maybe some whitespace, and new line,
and count indent levels. This will work surprisingly well, except few edge cases.

Thus - the 'Basic' part of the name - as opposed to 'Compiled' or 'AI assisted' or something
