## Description:
Both Listiary and Describe are Free and Open Source, licensed under [AGPL v3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html). In fact, they are copylefted, meaning that modified and derivative versions cannot simply be turned into proprietary software.
<br><br>
Admins, however, can host Listiary instances and profit from them through a [freemium model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freemium), by offering paid services such as private wiki hosting, support, and such services.
