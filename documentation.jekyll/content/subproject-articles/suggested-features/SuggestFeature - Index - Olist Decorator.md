## Description:
We have been focused on the mobile version of the wiki engine first,
because it is easier, and the right place to start, as it also works
as a full version, to an extent.
<br><br>
We ought to add some more complex navigation to the index,
and for one, there must be a mechanic where users not only open lists in
a new page, but also expand them as sublists - as we started to prototype this
to begin with - with our small lists on the full version.
<br><br>
The OPEN decorator does this - in the appropriate environment - that will probably be
the PC version (the full version) of the index, a list tagged with that decorator will
start opened to begin with.
