## Description:
I have this idea, that a user might use a zoomed out view on a mobile device,
but it can be difficult to click on a particular item, to interact with it,
or activate a link. So, I imagine a feature, where the user holds a finger over an item,
and then a magnifying glass rectangle appears under his finger, and the user moves his finger,
to highlight the item he wishes to interact with - highlighting appears, moving from item to item,
as the user moves his finger, and finally, when he lifts his finger - the selected item gets triggered.
