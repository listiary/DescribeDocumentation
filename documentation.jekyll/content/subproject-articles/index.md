<br>
Articles is a collection of community-written write-ups about Listiary and Describe. It is a place for feature suggestions, feature requests, bug reports, ideas, observations, and other contributions from the people who use and work with the project.
<br><br>
Every submission is turned into a short, browsable article using a common template. The original voice of the person who wrote it is preserved completely, including their wording, style, and even typos.
<br><br>
Articles are meant to be easy to browse, easy to reference, and useful over time. They provide a place where ideas and reports can live as part of the project's documentation without being rewritten into someone else's words.
<br><br>
