Sibling lists - those can be switched to
with an icon near the top - every sibling
list has an icon. If they are defined with a list,
that list's structure can be translated to those icons structure.
For our second example below, we can have icons order:

(2)(3)
   (4)
-------------------------------------------------------
list 1 ->

  :sibling <list2id>,
  :sibling <list3id>,

  some,
  items;

list 1 ->

  :siblings ->
    list2id, list3id,
    list4id;

  some,
  items;
