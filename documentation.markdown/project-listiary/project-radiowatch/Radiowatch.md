Radiowatch is a Listiary wiki, as its name and the Wikiproject Radiowatch label suggest. It is built for radio lovers: a place to browse playlists, explore programming schedules, check frequencies and stream URLs, and listen to favorite FM and web radio stations from around the world.

The wiki focuses on curating lists about radio stations, their broadcasts, playlists, shows, and technical details such as frequencies and streaming endpoints. Rather than being just a catalog, Radiowatch is meant to be browsable, listenable, and exploratory, inviting users to discover what is playing and how stations are structured.

From a Listiary developer’s point of view, Radiowatch is also a proving ground. It is an opportunity to experiment with plugin integration, observer bots, and data pipelines, and to see how a small number of plugins can effectively turn a Listiary wiki into a lightweight streaming and monitoring platform.

Radiowatch is still under development as of January 2026, but it may become the first publicly released Listiary wiki. You can follow its progress at [radiowatch.listiary.org](radiowatch.listiary.org).
