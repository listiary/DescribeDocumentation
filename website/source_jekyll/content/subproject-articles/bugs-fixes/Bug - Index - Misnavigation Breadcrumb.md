#### Original draft text :
If we include, for example `emergency stash I had before <.inve.es>` in our list,
then the navigation back breadcrumb points towards `Inventory <.inve>`, and not 
the original list we navigated from.<br><br>

```
Inventory <.inve> ->

    emergency stash I had before <.inve.es> ->

        rice (4 x 1 kg),
        pasta (2 x 500g),
        canned meat (2 x 300g),

        salt (1 x 1 kg);


    emergency stash to buy ideas <.inve.estb> ->

        beans, lentils, oats, pasta,
        cans of vegetables, cans of fruits, cans of meat,
        vitamin C, powdered milk, whey protein,
        sugar, honey, jam, chockolate,
        sunflower oil, olive oil, salt, baking soda, water,
        hard liquour in glass btttles wax sealed, coffee, tea, cigarettes,
        bateries, torch light;
```
