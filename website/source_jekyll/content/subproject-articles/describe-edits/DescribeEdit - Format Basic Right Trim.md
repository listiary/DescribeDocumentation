## Description:
We sometimes have white spaces before some end lines.
Those can happen by mistake, of by editing for the sake of recompilation, in the prototyping and testing of Listiary.
Regardless, a white space at the end of the line, before the new line sequence is highly unlikely to be significant, and we can just remove it, without even parsing the document.

Thus - the 'Basic' part of the name - as opposed to 'Compiled' or 'AI assisted' or something
