## Description:

I believe that there should be a way for users to donate towards the development or upkeep
of a single list/article, or a bunch.<br>

Basically, imagine if 3 editors and 5 users write and evaluate the integrity of a tree of articles,
because the community believes they are very important, and is paying for the work.<br>

We might call that subsidized articles.
