We’re looking for a few things right now — donations, of course, but also a small core team of around three or four people. The tech stack is pretty simple: mostly vanilla JS, PHP, and a bit of SQL. The compiler’s in C#, but I’ve got that part covered.

I’m not hung up on titles or certificates. What matters is that you can work independently and take ownership of what you build. Total beginners or folks who constantly need guidance probably won’t be a good fit for this stage.

We also really need a writer — someone who can handle blog posts, social media, PR, and help with fundraising. A graphic designer would be great too; we might hire a freelancer for that, but we definitely need a logo and some basic branding to start.

The core team will be paid — at first symbolically or not much at all, but the plan is to move toward something more sustainable over time.

Our workflow is intentionally slow-paced and flexible. People contribute when they can, take breaks when they need to, and come back refreshed. This project is about building something out of love for the craft, and pride in creating and owning free and open-source software. If you’d rather do something else for a while, that’s totally fine — don’t force yourself to code when it doesn’t feel right.
