stewp
(noun)

A dish that exists halfway between a soup and a stew — a soup so thick it could pass for a stew, or a stew that’s suspiciously runny.

The inevitable result when you get a little too generous with the ingredients while making soup.

Example:

“I was going for spinach and egg soup, but it turned into a stewp.”

Origin:
Blend of stew and soup, similar in logic to brunch.
